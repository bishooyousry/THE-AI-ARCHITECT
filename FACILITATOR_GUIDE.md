# Facilitator guide — THE AI ARCHITECT: Design Your Assistant (online)

**Goal:** participants can define the **Role, Scope, Inputs and Outputs** of an AI assistant for their own job *before* building one for real. The blueprint they produce is reused in the next activity, Build Your Own Assistant.
**Format:** individual, 8–10 minutes of play (the classroom version was 6–8 minutes) + 5–7 minutes of debrief.

## Know the source before you present
- The course notes give the goal, the 4 boxes, the example “Meeting Notes Assistant”, the tip that **Scope is the most important box**, and the two debrief questions below (questions 1 and 2 are from the course notes). The worksheet handout is not in the deck I received, so the template content in the game is illustrative.
- This activity produces a **design blueprint**, not a working assistant. Say so out loud.

## Before the session (5 min)
- Open the link → Trainer (PIN 2026). One session per group (**+ New session**).
- Put the **player link** (or the 5-character code) in the chat. An Arabic link is available.
- Tell participants: use **made-up or approved, non-sensitive information only** — no real customer, employee or restricted data. Sound is optional (🔊). Works on phones.

## Run (timing)
| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Say: “Before we build a real AI assistant, let’s design one first — it makes the building step much faster. Think of one task in your job. If you’re stuck, start from the Meeting Notes template.” Share link/code. |
| 0:01 | Everyone is in. Optionally open **Projector view** (assistants appear as they are named). |
| 0:01–0:10 | Watch the table: assistant names and purposes, the four system lights, boundary-test score, blueprint completeness. Expand a row (⌄) to read a full blueprint. |
| ~0:10 | Ask 2–3 volunteers to share just their **Scope**. Then **Reveal leaderboard**. |
| 0:10–0:17 | Debrief (below). Remind everyone to **keep the blueprint** (Copy / Download). |

## What happens in the game (for narration)
1. **Initialization** — name, purpose, avatar; the assistant appears as an incomplete hologram.
2. **Four systems** — Role (job type, user, one-sentence job), **Scope** (arrange capabilities into allowed / must NOT / needs human approval, then add your own boundary), Inputs (use / anonymize / never share), Outputs (format, length, quality, human review).
3. **Boundary test** — three requests built from the participant’s own design; consequences of loose boundaries are explained.
4. **Blueprint** — editable fields + generated assistant instructions for ChatGPT / Copilot / Gemini (starting point only; features differ by platform and company policy).
5. **Activation** — the avatar powers on; the screen states it is a design, not a deployed assistant.

## Debrief questions
1. *(Course notes)* **Which box was easiest to fill in? Which was hardest?** (Dashboard → Systems completed: the lowest bar is the one fewest people finished.)
2. *(Course notes)* **What is one boundary — something the assistant should NOT do — that you added to Scope?**
3. *(Suggested)* In your boundary test, which request surprised you? Where was your Scope too loose or too strict?
4. *(Suggested)* What is the difference between “outside scope” and “needs human approval” in your assistant? Give one example of each.
5. *(Suggested)* Which of your inputs must be anonymized or never shared? What placeholder would you use?
6. *(Suggested)* What will you do first with this blueprint in the next activity — which parts become the assistant’s instructions, and which become knowledge files?

## Notes
- Scoring is rule-based and shown to the player. Use it for feedback, not for grading people.
- Progress reaches the dashboard when a system is locked, when a phase changes, and at submission — a designer may look idle while typing.
- If the live connection drops: **Refresh** on the dashboard rebuilds from the relay history; otherwise collect backup files and use **Import**.
- Export CSV at the end if you want the blueprints (role, scope, exclusions, inputs, outputs, review checkpoints).
